How to read the standings
Rank Pts
Matchup wins plus weekly top-five finishes.
Top 5
Weeks finishing in the top half of the league.
PF / PA
Points scored and points allowed.
xW-xL
Expected record based on each weekly score versus the full field.
Luck
Actual wins minus expected wins; positive means favorable results.
SOS
Average points per game of remaining opponents.

Playoff Odds

Monte Carlo simulation over the remaining regular-season matchups using each team's average weekly score. For entertainment only — assumes lineups and rosters stay constant. Odds are capped at 1–99% until a team mathematically clinches a playoff spot or is eliminated.
