### Resolve future conditional picks

The confirmed 2026 AYP/S/T conditional has already been resolved in `data/draft_picks.json`, so it will not appear as unresolved in the tool.

For later conditionals:

1. Log in to **My Team** as GSA and open **Commissioner**.
2. Open **Resolve Conditional** and select the unresolved condition.
3. Review the displayed claimant, candidate picks, original teams, and current owners.
4. Choose the pick that conveys and confirm its final owner.
5. Enter the result or determining facts in **Resolution details** and confirm the action.

The panel reads unresolved conditions directly from the authoritative `data/draft_picks.json` source, rather than relying on a potentially stale website export. The action changes ownership only for the selected pick, preserves that pick's ownership history, leaves the other candidates with their current owners, clears the resolved condition from all candidates, and records the full decision in the commissioner audit log. A duplicate submission is rejected after the condition has been cleared.
